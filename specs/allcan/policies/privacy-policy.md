How ALL CAN Sport collects, uses and protects personal information.

- **Applies to:** allcansport.ca and every ALL CAN Sport program
- **Effective:** [TBC]

## Our commitment

Protecting personal information is part of how we run ALL CAN Sport. ALL CAN Sport is a division of **Athlete Institute, Ltd.** ("Athlete Institute", "we", "us"). We handle personal information in line with Canada's *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act* (PIPEDA) and the other laws that apply to us. Every staff member follows this policy when handling personal information.

## What this policy covers

This policy applies to all personal information we hold about players, parents and guardians, website visitors, coaches, job applicants and partners, whether it reaches us through allcansport.ca, our programs, email, phone or text message.

**Personal information** is information about an identifiable person, such as a name, contact details, date of birth, health information needed for safe participation, or payment details.

## What we collect

**Information you give us**

- Parent or guardian name, email and, if you choose, phone number.
- A player's name, age group or birth year, and program choices.
- Requests and enquiries, such as a request for a new location or an offer to host a program.
- Financial assistance requests. These are kept confidential and separate from program and coaching records.
- Your account details when you sign in (name and email), the teams you follow and your notification choices.
  
**Information about players in our programs**

- Name, team, division, jersey number, schedules and results.
- Health and safety information only where it's needed for safe participation, including records required by Ontario's *Rowan's Law* when a player is removed from or returns to sport. Access is limited to the people who need it.
- Photos and video, only as described under Youth players and photos.
  
**Information collected automatically**

- Pages visited, counted in total. Our website analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) doesn't use cookies and doesn't follow you across other sites.
- Server logs, including IP addresses, kept to run and secure the site. We don't link them to your identity.
  
**Ask ALL CAN**

Questions typed into Ask ALL CAN are processed by an AI service to answer them from our published pages. Please don't include personal information. We keep questions it couldn't answer, without any information about who asked, to improve the site.

## How we use it

We use personal information only to run ALL CAN Sport and to understand what our families need:

- Running programs: placement, teams, schedules, results and communication with families.
- Telling you when registration opens for a program you asked about.
- Sending the notifications you chose, such as schedule changes and final scores.
- Answering requests and enquiries, and reviewing financial assistance requests.
- Taking registrations and payments, once registration opens on this site.
- Keeping the site secure and working well.
- Meeting legal obligations, including Rowan's Law and tax records.
We won't use personal information for any other purpose without your consent.

## Consent

We ask for your consent when we collect your information, for example by a checkbox on a form, and we explain what it's for. A parent or guardian gives consent for players under 18.

We send marketing emails and texts only with your express consent, as Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) requires. You can withdraw consent at any time: use the unsubscribe link in any email, reply STOP to a text, or contact us. If you withdraw consent, we may not be able to provide some services; for example, we can't place a player without a way to reach their family.

## Youth players and photos

- A youth player's name appears on public rosters only with a parent or guardian's permission. Otherwise they appear as "Team member".
- Public pages never show a player's date of birth, school, contact details or private evaluations.
- Player profile pages are kept out of search engines.
- We publish photos and video of youth players only with a parent or guardian's media permission. You can ask us to remove one at any time.

## Who we share it with

We never sell, rent or trade personal information or contact lists.

We share it only with service providers that help us run ALL CAN Sport, under agreements that require them to protect it and use it only for that work:

- **Vercel**: website hosting and visit counts.
- **Supabase**: our database.
- **Clerk**: sign-in to your Athlete Institute account.
- **Resend**: email.
- **Twilio**: text messages.
- **Stripe**: payments, once registration opens. We never see or store full card numbers.
- **Anthropic**: the AI service behind Ask ALL CAN and our staff writing tools.
Athlete Institute staff who run ALL CAN programs see the information they need for their work. We may share overall statistics that can't identify anyone with partners.

We may disclose personal information without consent only where the law allows it, such as:

- when required by law or for an investigation by the proper authorities;
- in an emergency that threatens someone's life, health or safety;
- to investigate a breach of an agreement.

## Where it's stored

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States. While it's there, it's subject to the laws of that country, which may allow authorities there to access it.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires. Then we delete or destroy it securely.

## How we protect it

- Only staff who need personal information for their job can see it.
- Our systems are protected by strong passwords and access controls.
- Every page and form on allcansport.ca uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Payments are handled by Stripe.
- Paper records are kept in locked cabinets, and older records in secure storage.
- We don't send personal information to volunteers, students, interns or other unpaid staff by email or other electronic means.
If a privacy breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll tell the people affected and report it to the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as the law requires. We keep a record of every breach.

## Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and the site working. We don't use advertising cookies, and our analytics doesn't use cookies at all.

## Text messages

If you give us a mobile number and agree to texts, Athlete Institute, Ltd. may text you about ALL CAN Sport programs, such as schedule changes, cancellations and reminders. Message and data rates may apply. How often we text varies. Reply **STOP** at any time to opt out, or **HELP** for help.

We never share mobile numbers or texting consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotions.

## Links to other sites

Our site links to other sites, including other Athlete Institute sites, partners and Ontario government pages. We're not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their policies.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction:** send us a written request and we'll show you the personal information we hold about you, after confirming your identity. If something is wrong, tell us and we'll correct it.
- **If we refuse:** in rare cases the law lets us refuse access. We'll explain why in writing, and you can challenge the decision.
- **Withdraw consent or unsubscribe:** at any time, as described under Consent.
- **Concerns:** contact us first and we'll investigate and respond. You can also complain to the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca).
