# Youth and rec league websites: what to borrow for allcansport.ca

Research date: Oct 27 2026. Method: web search only. Direct page fetching was blocked from the research environment, so these notes come from search-indexed page text, not screenshots. Some indexed pages are older snapshots; treat specific numbers as examples, not current facts.

---

## 1. What the leading sites do

### Brodie League (brodierec.com)
- **Location-first structure.** A locations index lists every city across Canada and the US; each venue has its own page ([locations](https://www.brodierec.com/locations), [Toronto Downtown](https://www.brodierec.com/locations/toronto-downtown), [Ottawa](https://www.brodierec.com/locations/ottawa-ontario)).
- **Honest city status.** Cities that aren't open are marked **waitlist**, with a dedicated waitlist page that offers an incentive and states when registration opens ([New Jersey waitlist](https://www.brodierec.com/join-waitlist/new-jersey)).
- **Venue pages carry the facts:** venue name and address, the night it runs, the time window, the divisions offered at that venue, and the registration close date.
- **Named seasons** on a fixed rhythm (four 12-week seasons a year), so families learn the calendar.
- **Skill divisions** (D6 to D1) explained once, then each venue lists which it offers.
- **Two registration paths:** register a team, or register as a free agent and be placed at your level ([about](https://www.brodierec.com/company/about)).
- **Newsroom** separate from league pages ([news example](https://www.brodierec.com/news/new-leagues-new-tables-same-house)); FAQ and a dedicated refund policy page ([FAQ](https://www.brodierec.com/faq)); an offers page.
- Caveat from the research: venue pages are not fully consistent with each other, which is the same accuracy problem the ALL CAN audit flags. A single program record avoids it.

### i9 Sports (i9sports.com)
- **"Find a league near you"** is the homepage's primary action ([home](https://www.i9sports.com/)); city landing pages exist per market ([example](https://www.i9sports.com/bakersfield-youth-sports-leagues)).
- Program value is stated as a **short list of what's included**: age-appropriate instruction, a weekly sportsmanship lesson, no tryouts or drafts, everyone plays, practice right before the game on the same day, trained officials.
- Parent trust signals: coaches background-checked, schedules and standings online at any time.

### SportsEngine Play, SportsOne, Little League, USA Hockey
- Program discovery is filtered by **location, sport and age**, with a "near me" option ([SportsEngine Play](https://discover.sportsengineplay.com/), [SportsOne](https://sportsone.org/)). This matches your location → sport → league hierarchy.

### Steve Nash Youth Basketball / Canada Basketball
- Development is described in **stages**: physical literacy first, then basketball skills (dribbling, shooting, rebounding), game-based and fun-first, built on Long-Term Athlete Development, with life skills such as teamwork and goal setting ([Canada Basketball](https://www.basketball.ca/news/why-is-steve-nash-youth-basketball-so-great-for-kids)).
- Useful for the parent guide: explain what each age is learning and why.

### Jr. NBA / USA Basketball age standards
- Published **age-appropriate equipment and rules**: smaller balls and lower rims for younger groups, no zone and no three-point shot for ages 7 to 11 ([NBA/USA Basketball standards](https://jr.nba.com/nba-usa-basketball-announce-age-appropriate-rules-standards-youth-basketball-competitions)). Canada's Rookie Division for ages 5 to 7 uses smaller balls and lowered hoops ([Basketball BC](https://basketball.bc.ca/blog/post/nba-rolls-out-jr-nba-youth-program-and-rookie-division-in-canada/)).
- Useful for Small Ball: a short "how the game is adapted" panel per age group.

### Upward Sports (church-hosted leagues)
- Parent FAQs walk through **evaluation → team placement → when you'll hear from the coach**, and a weekly rhythm of one practice and one game ([Woodside FAQ](https://woodsidebible.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/2022-Parent-FAQ-Basketball.pdf), [Redland Baptist](https://redlandbaptist.org/upward)).
- Useful for the house league: publish the placement process and the date families will be contacted.

### Overtime Elite
- Indexed coverage shows team-based rosters, coach-led teams and published schedules, but the official player directory itself could not be read. The audit's description (searchable player list, linked team and profile pages) is the reference.

### Family apps: TeamSnap, GameChanger
- Parents expect to **follow a team** for free and get **score and schedule notifications**; score alerts fire only when the score changes ([TeamSnap Live](https://www.teamsnap.com/company/press-releases/teamsnap-releases-teamsnap-live-for-real-time-updates-for-youth-sports-game), [GameChanger](https://gc.com/app-features)).
- Useful for Compete on the site: "Follow this team" for signed-in families, with schedule-change and final-score notifications through the portal's `notify()`.

### Private instruction pages
- M14's page could not be retrieved. Comparable basketball training pages publish **per-session price, multi-session packs, session length, group-size pricing and a direct booking link** ([Genesis Hoops](https://genesishoops.pike13.com/appointments/78386), [Hoop Work](https://hoopwork.playbookapi.com/home/individual.html)).

### Financial assistance
- Ontario clubs link families to **KidSport Ontario** and **Canadian Tire Jumpstart** alongside their own policy, explain eligibility and timing, and keep the application confidential ([Ottawa Lions](https://ottawalions.com/financial-assistance), [Newmarket Minor Hockey](https://nmha.ca/Pages/14613/Financial_Assistance/)). Processing can take weeks and funding isn't guaranteed, so pages state that plainly.

### General guidance
- Families should be able to **register, pay and find schedules without leaving the site**, and most of them do it on a phone ([JerseyWatch, vendor source](https://www.jerseywatch.com/blog/youth-sports-website-best-practices)).
- Publish **registration windows and deadlines early**, including early-bird, open, jersey-order and first-practice dates ([YMCA example](https://www.gwrymca.org/programs/youth-sports-leagues-clinics)).
- Staff and coach pages help parents compare programs ([JerseyWatch roundup](https://www.jerseywatch.com/blog/best-youth-sports-websites)).

## 2. Best practices to build in

1. **Location is the front door.** The first choice is the city; every other page knows which city the visitor picked and remembers it.
2. **Status on every location and program:** open, opening soon, waitlist, full, closed. Waitlist and opening-soon pages capture interest and say when registration opens.
3. **One fact block per program**, always in the same order: ages or birth years, day and time, dates with year, venue, full price with taxes, what's included, spots left, deadline.
4. **Season calendar** per location: early-bird, registration open, placement, uniform, first session, last session, playoffs.
5. **Two registration paths where they apply:** team and free agent for adults; individual and sibling or friend request for youth.
6. **Placement explained:** how teams are formed, when families hear from a coach.
7. **Age-adapted play explained** for Small Ball and younger divisions: ball size, rim height, format.
8. **Coaches visible** on city and program pages, with screening stated.
9. **Schedules, standings and rosters on the same site**, filtered to the family's city and division, with a "follow this team" option and change notifications.
10. **Parent guide and FAQ** covering equipment, uniforms, attendance, weather and cancellations, refunds, behaviour, photo consent.
11. **Refund and cancellation policy** on its own page, linked from every price.
12. **Financial assistance beside the price**, confidential, linking KidSport Ontario and Jumpstart.
13. **News separate from evergreen pages**, tagged by city, sport and program, every post dated.
14. **Retire expired content automatically**: when a program's season ends, its page switches to next-season interest capture.
15. **Phone first:** sticky registration action on program pages, schedules readable without zooming, tables that scroll inside their box.

## 3. Recommended sitemap (location → sport → league)

```
/                                   Home: city picker, sports, what's on now
/locations                          All cities + venues, status per city
/[city]                             City hub: sports offered here, venues, coaches, calendar
/[city]/[sport]                     Sport in that city: every league type offered
/[city]/[sport]/[program]           Program page (live data, register)
/programs                           Cross-city program finder (filters: city, sport, type, age)
/[sport]                            Sport overview: pathway (skills → league → circuit), formats
/circuit                            ALL CAN Circuit (rep): teams, events, selection
/scores                             Compete: schedules + results, filtered by city/sport/division
/standings, /standings/[division]   Compete standings
/teams, /teams/[team]               Team list + team page (roster, schedule, results)
/players, /players/[player]         Player directory + profile (consent rules)
/training                           Skills + private instruction (formats, coaches, book)
/stories, /stories/[slug]           Newsroom: announcements + features
/parent-guide                       How it works, placement, equipment, policies, FAQ
/financial-assistance               Policy, limits, confidential application
/about, /contact, /policies         Operated by Athlete Institute; refund, privacy, conduct
/shop                               Links out to Gear
/watch, /tickets                    Link out to Live and Tickets
```

Concrete examples: `/orangeville/basketball/youth-league`, `/orangeville/basketball/small-ball`, `/orangeville/volleyball/skills`, `/orangeville/basketball/mens-league`.
